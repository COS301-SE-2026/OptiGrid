Energy intelligence for every building you operate.
Ingest meter data, monitor live consumption, detect anomalies, forecast demand, and act on cost-saving recommendations. One platform for every building in your portfolio.
Get started Book a demo

Capabilities

Multi-source ingestion

CSV, JSON, and live API feeds across every building.

Real-time monitoring

Live kWh, peak load, and cost per building in one dashboard.

Benchmark performance

Side-by-side EUI and cost comparisons across the portfolio.

Anomaly detection

Automated alerts on abnormal spikes, drops, and patterns.

Demand forecasting

ML predictions with weather and occupancy inputs to plan procurement.

Optimisation engine

Load shifting and off-peak suggestions with quantified savings.

How it works

1. Connect

Upload CSV, point at an API, or stream JSON from a meter.

2. Visualise

See live consumption and historical trends per building.

3. Detect

Get alerted on anomalies the moment they happen.

4. Optimise

Act on forecast-backed recommendations.

Built for teams
Multi-tenant by design, with role-based access for viewers, managers, and admins. Deploy across an organisation without giving everyone the keys.

wf-19-recommendation-detailRecommendation Detail

Shift HVAC startup to 06:30

Sandton HQ · Estimated savings R 4,200/mo

DismissMark as planned
Rationale

Current startup at 05:00 draws 38 kWh before occupants arrive. Shifting to 06:30 reduces pre-occupancy idle load by 60% without affecting comfort at 07:00.

Source data

90 days of HVAC sub-meter readings + occupancy sensor data.

Action plan
  1. Update BMS schedule to start HVAC at 06:30.
  2. Monitor indoor temperature at 07:00 for 7 days.
  3. Roll back if comfort complaints exceed baseline.
